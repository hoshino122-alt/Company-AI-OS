# DAY33 AI Employee Growth Management

## Overview

DAY33 introduces the **AI Employee Growth Management System** into Company AI OS.

AI employees are no longer simple task executors.
Every completed job becomes experience that contributes to continuous growth.

This system records accumulated experience, analyzes employee performance, and stores growth history inside the Knowledge Base.

As AI employees gain experience, they become increasingly capable of producing higher-quality work.

---

## Features

* Experience Point Management
* AI Employee Skill Growth
* Performance Analysis
* Knowledge Base Integration
* Continuous Learning Workflow

---

## Presentation Engine Update

DAY33 also improves the presentation system.

The slide rendering process has been redesigned:

* AI Image Generator → Background creation
* Ren'Py → Titles, Company logo, DAY number, captions

This separation creates a consistent visual design while making future maintenance much easier.
