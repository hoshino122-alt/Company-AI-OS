# DAY35 - AI Core Version 0.1

## Overview

Today marks a major milestone for the Company AI OS project.

The first version of the **AI Core** has been implemented.

The AI Core will become the central component of Company AI OS, managing future modules such as AI Employees, Memory, Workflow, and AI Agents.

Version 0.1 focuses on creating the foundation rather than implementing advanced functionality.

---

## Implemented

* AI Core Version 0.1
* Initial Boot Sequence
* Core initialization screen
* Version management
* Automatic startup animation
* Project architecture for future expansion

---

## Design Concept

The AI Core is designed as the "heart" of Company AI OS.

Instead of developing individual AI features independently, every future subsystem will communicate through the AI Core.

This architecture provides:

* Centralized AI management
* Scalable module integration
* Consistent system behavior
* Easier future maintenance
